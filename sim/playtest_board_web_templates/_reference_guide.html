Mechanics Glossary

  • Sunder: +1 DMG this round and all subsequent rounds of this pull.
  • Persistent: Gain block this round, and carry an automatic block into the NEXT round. (Echo).
  • Elusive: Evade exactly one melee attack this round. Does not work against Ranged.
  • Thorns: Deal 2 or 3 DMG automatically, unless you are At Range this round.
  • Ruthless: If your attack deals lethal damage, prevent all damage taken this round.
  • Pierce: Ignore up to 2 enemy block.

Town Upgrades & Costs

  • Food: 4g (Heals to full HP)
  • Potion: 3g (Heals 8 HP. Takes 1 Bag slot)
  • Bag Upgrade: 12g (Unlocks 6 slots. Requires taking 1 Level 2 Quest)
  • Trainer Skill: 8g (Buy randomly drawn Level 2 class cards). Needs 6 XP (Level 2); not available at Level 1.
  • Blacksmith repair: dying breaks all your equipped gear. Repair it at the Town Forge for Gold equal to the item's level (Level 1 item = 1g, Level 2 item = 2g).

Quests

  • Level 1 start: you hold 3 quests: 2 loot quests and 1 kill quest, picked in the Quest Draft. Together they are worth 6 XP, which is Level 2. You get no replacement as each is turned in.
  • Loot quests: carry the matching loot tokens to Town. They take Bag space. Gold ladder 4 / 2 / 1 / 0, falling one step each trip the quest is still open.
  • Kill quests: defeat mobs instead of carrying loot. No Bag space, no token; progress is kept on the card between trips. 2 XP, Gold ladder 2 / 1 / 0 / 0, and it decays one step per trip left open, like a loot quest.
  • What counts as a kill: any outright win against a roster mob (Quest Node pull, Blind Pull, Scouted Pull at a Border, Scroll of Vanquishing). Fleeing, Smoke Bombs and Spice encounters do not count. Dying never erases progress.
  • Filters: a role named on the card, or Favored / Struggles (the mobs on your Class Guide lists; they are shown on the card). One kill advances every active quest it qualifies for, but fills only one clause of a two-clause card.
  • Commissioned Veteran (14 XP): the Zone 3 and 4 bounty boards close to you. Quests you already hold stay playable but are not replaced when turned in. Report to the Zone 4 Trainer for the Citadel Commission and the Tier Gate Boss.
  • Scout Token: spend it on a pull or on challenging the Tier Gate Boss to draw 2 cards and pick 1.

Material Drops by Zone

  • Tier 1 Materials (Zones 1 & 2): Crag-Iron, Scavenged Pelt, Snap-Root
  • Tier 2 Materials (Zones 3 & 4): Sun-Copper, Bristle-Pelt, River-Mint
